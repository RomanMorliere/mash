Paris x Rotterdam

We mash underground culture across cities.

MASH is a collective at the intersection of music, fashion, and art. Our mission is to become a gateway between Paris and Rotterdam by creating hybrid events, moving artists across borders, and translating one city's energy into another.

Who We Are

We are a Paris-Rotterdam based collective building experiences where scenes collide. From vernissages with multiple concepts under one roof to curated lineups that travel between cities, MASH designs cultural bridges.

Music

DJ curation, sonic identities, and club-ready programming.

Fashion

Styling, performance looks, and collective visual direction.

Art

Installations, vernissage concepts, and city storytelling.

Why Work With MASH

Cross-city Access

We activate trusted networks in both Paris and Rotterdam scenes.

Concept Design

Every event is built as a mashup: formats, artists, audiences, and aesthetics.

Venue Fit

We adapt lineups and programming to your space while keeping underground credibility.
